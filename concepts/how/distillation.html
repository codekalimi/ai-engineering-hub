How does distillation work?

In one lineA large "teacher" model generates high-quality outputs, and a smaller "student" model is fine-tuned on them, inheriting much of the teacher's ability on that task at a fraction of the cost.

How to read this diagram

Flow diagram: read left to right. Dashed loops show steps that repeat; ↓ joins rows that follow on from each other.

Step by step
  1. Collect realistic prompts for the task you care about.
  2. Run them through a large, capable teacher model.
  3. Filter the outputs for quality (automatic checks or review).
  4. Fine-tune a smaller student model on the teacher's outputs.
  5. Evaluate the student against the teacher on held-out examples.
Key details
  • Works best for narrow, high-volume tasks.
  • Check the teacher provider's terms; some restrict training other models on outputs.
