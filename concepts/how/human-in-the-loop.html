How does human-in-the-loop approval work (interrupt and resume)?

In one lineThe graph pauses at a node, saves its state, and returns the pending action to your app. When a person decides, the app resumes the same thread with their answer.

Step by step
  1. Your app invokes the graph with a thread_id.
  2. A node calls interrupt() with the action that needs approval, e.g. an email draft.
  3. The state is saved by the checkpointer, and invoke returns the interrupt payload.
  4. Your UI shows it to a person, who approves, edits or rejects it.
  5. Your app invokes again with Command(resume=decision) and the same thread_id.
  6. The graph loads the saved state and continues; interrupt() returns the person's decision.
Key details
  • It requires a checkpointer, and the wait can last seconds or days.
  • On resume, the node runs again from its start: keep side effects after the interrupt call.
