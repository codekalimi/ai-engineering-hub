How does LLM-as-a-judge work?

In one lineA strong model is given the question, the answer and a rubric (and sometimes a reference answer), and returns a structured verdict with a reason; it's checked against human labels before you trust it.

How to read this diagram

Step by step
  1. Write a rubric with clear, narrow criteria, e.g. "Is every claim supported by the context? yes/no".
  2. Give the judge the question, the answer, any reference, and a few graded examples.
  3. Ask for structured output: a score plus a short reason.
  4. Compare judge scores with human labels on a sample, and adjust the rubric until they agree.
Key details
  • Known biases: preferring the first option, longer answers, or its own style.
  • For A/B comparisons, run both orders and average.
  • Yes/no criteria are more reliable than 1–10 scales.
